Questions

Before you call, you might be wondering—

Is the $750/month the whole cost, or is there more?

The $750 is my management fee. You also fund your own ad spend directly with Google — I don't mark that up or take a cut of it. We agree on that number together before your campaign launches, so there's no surprise bill.

How fast will I see results?

Your site can be live within about a week. Google Ads typically takes 2–4 weeks to find its footing as it learns which searches turn into real calls — that's why I ask for a 30-day window before judging results.

What if it just doesn't work for my business?

Then you cancel after 30 days and owe nothing further. I'd genuinely rather find that out fast than have you stuck paying for something that isn't bringing in jobs.

Do you only work with plumbers and HVAC companies?

Those are common, but I work with most home service trades — electrical, roofing, landscaping, garage doors, pest control, and similar. If people search for it when something in their house breaks, it's probably a fit.

What's your service area?

I focus on one metro area at a time so I actually know it well, instead of spreading thin everywhere. Reach out and I'll tell you straight away if I'm currently taking clients where you're located.
